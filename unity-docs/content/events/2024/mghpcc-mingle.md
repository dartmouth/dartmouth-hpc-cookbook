Attending PEARC’24 in Providence, RI? Join the MGHPCC, including Unity staff, for a Monday late-afternoon, post-conference snack break and chat with MGHPCC staff, professionals from the MGHPCC campuses, and friends. No registration is required to attend. We hope to see you there!

The Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC), located less than 85 miles from PEARC’24, is a collaborative data center that brings together the vibrant research computing community in New England. This event will be a gathering of MGHPCC associated personnel, including cyberinfrastructure professionals, researchers, students, and friends of the community. Join us for light refreshments, (human) networking, and social activities with the MGHPCC staff and community members.

*Monday, July 22, 2024 5:00 PM-7:00 PM ET in the 5th level East Pre-Function Area.*
