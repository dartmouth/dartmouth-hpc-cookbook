[This event](https://www.cssi.umass.edu/events/fri-09272024-1200/introduction-unity-workshop) will be hybrid, **located on Zoom and in Lederle Graduate Research Center (LGRC) A112**. If you would like to join on Zoom, please [register here](https://umass-amherst.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJEkf-uoqT8iEtAUQmicIcLsEdDbeu9yNLsr).

Join us to learn how you can leverage the Unity Platform for social science research! Unity is well suited for social science research projects involving data from small to large scale. This workshop is meant as a general onboarding workshop with a specific focus on tools for social science research, and requires **NO PRIOR UNITY EXPERIENCE** to attend.  Motivated attendees can [register in advance for a Unity account]({{< param account-base-url >}}), but it is not required for this event.   Bring your questions and curiosity.  All are welcome!

Lunch will be served in Lederle Graduate Research Center (LGRC) A112.
