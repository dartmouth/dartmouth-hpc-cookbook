*This workshop is presented by the [University of Rhode Island AI Lab]({{< relref "news/2024/02/uri-spring-workshops" >}}).*

Over the past several decades, decreasing sequencing costs have resulted in the generation of vast amounts of biological data, providing researchers with the opportunity to tackle complex biological questions but also presenting a great computational challenge. Many analyses now are performed on high performance computing clusters such as Unity which offer enough computational power to work with such data. This workshop will offer a practical introduction to how to get started with conducting bioinformatics analyses on Unity and take participants through all of the steps required to run a program on Unity.
