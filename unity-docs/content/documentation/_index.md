Welcome to Unity!
The Unity platform is a collaborative, multi-institutional high-performance computing platform located at the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center [(MGHPCC)](https://www.mghpcc.org). Unity is under active development and supports primarily research activities. Partnering institutions currently include UMass Amherst, University of Rhode Island, UMass Dartmouth,
UMass Lowell, UMass Boston, and Mount Holyoke College. If you need help, please
[contact us]({{< relref "contact" >}}).
