{{< iconcard "forum" "Join our Unity Community Slack" >}}
The Unity facilitation team maintains a vibrant Unity User Community on Slack.
This community is the best place to get (non-critical) cluster announcements
and chat with the Unity team and fellow Unity users. To join, review our
guidelines and follow the join instructions on the
[Unity User Community page]({{< relref "community" >}}).
{{< /iconcard >}}

{{< iconcard "devices" "Attend office hours" >}}
Come chat with a Unity facilitator at our weekly live office hours on Zoom!
Office hours are held **every Tuesday from 2:30 pm to 4 pm Eastern time**.
Office hours are not held on [UMass Amherst campus closure days or holidays](https://www.umass.edu/hr/benefits-and-pay/benefits/paid-time-and-holidays/holidays-and-campus-closure-days).
Join us on Tuesdays [at this link]({{< param officehours-url >}}).
{{< /iconcard >}}

{{< iconcard "mail" "Submit a ticket" >}}
For inquiries about purchasing, software installation,
questions with private research information, or if you prefer getting
help via email, submit a ticket by emailing
[{{< param "help-email" >}}](mailto:{{< param "help-email" >}}). For a faster resolution, follow these [guidelines on how to ask for help]({{< relref "documentation/help/asking-questions" >}}).
{{< /iconcard >}}

{{< iconcard "help" "Request software" >}}
If you would like software installed to Unity, you can request it by completing our Software Request Form. To do so, log into the [Unity portal]({{< param account-base-url>}}) and select "Software Install Request Form" from the side-menu.
{{< /iconcard >}}

{{< iconcard "school" "Ask institution-specific Unity questions" >}}
If you have a question about the specifics of your institution's Unity policy,
please email your campus contact from
[our point of contact list]({{< relref "people#point-of-contact" >}}).
{{< /iconcard >}}

{{< iconcard "help" "Inquire about industry or academic partnerships" >}}
If you would like to inquire about joining Unity as an industry or academic
partner, email Thomas Bernardin at
[tbernard@umass.edu](mailto:tbernard@umass.edu).
{{< /iconcard >}}
