## Happy holidays, Unity Community!

The Unity facilitation and systems administration teams
will have reduced services and help hours from Monday, December 18, 2023, until
Tuesday, January 2, 2024.
During this
time, please allow additional time for responses on help tickets and
the [Unity User Community Slack]({{< relref community >}}).
In addition, **we will not be holding Zoom office hours
until the UMass Amherst Spring semester begins on February 1, 2024**.
The first session of Zoom office hours in
2024 is Tuesday, February 6, 2024, from 2:30 pm to 4 pm EST. If you need a
"face-to-face" meeting during this time, please email {{< help-email >}}.

## Upcoming events

We have two upcoming events on the schedule. Tomorrow (Friday, December 15, 2023),
we're hosting a [Snakemake workshop]({{< relref "events/2023/snakemake" >}}) at 1 pm EST.
Our [Spring onboarding session]({{< relref "events/2024/spring-onboarding" >}})
is scheduled for Tuesday, February 13, 2024, at 1 pm EST. We hope you'll join us
for our workshops!

## Unity User Community guidelines and Slack Pro

We've launched [new Unity User Community guidelines]({{< relref "contact/community" >}})
aimed at maintaining a high-quality and inclusive community Slack. Please review the guidelines
and note that participation in our user Slack indicates an agreement to adhere to the guidelines.
In addition, we've upgraded to Slack Pro! All chat history is now searchable.
