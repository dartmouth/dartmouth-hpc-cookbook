The [University of Rhode Island's Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://web.uri.edu/ai/)
invites the Unity community to
attend the following data and computational science-focused workshops.
The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research
and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common
AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects.
For questions regarding the URI AI Lab workshops, contact
[Indrani Mandal](mailto:indrani_mandal@uri.edu?subject=URI%20AI%20Lab%20Workshops).
