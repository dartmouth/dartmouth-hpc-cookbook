## ANNOUNCEMENTS 📢

### 📸 Snapshots for `/project`
- In addition to `/work` and `/home`, users can now recover files for `/projects`
- [See more info about snapshots]({{< relref "documentation/cluster_specs/storage/#snapshots" >}}).

### 📥 More Space in `/scratch3`
- We now have 500T more scratch space available in `/scratch3`!

### 🛜 Ethernet Speed Features
- For jobs with network speed requirements, you can now specify 10gbps, 25gbps, 40gbps, or 100gbps.
- [See network speed options]({{< relref "documentation/cluster_specs/features/#network-speed" >}}).

### ❄️ Reminder: Winter Break Reduced Help Hours 12/23/24 to 1/5/25
- The Unity facilitation and systems administration teams will have reduced services and help hours from December 23, 2024, until January 5, 2025.
