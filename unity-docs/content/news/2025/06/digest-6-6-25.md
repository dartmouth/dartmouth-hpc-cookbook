## ✅ Annual maintenance is complete!
As you may have already heard, Unity is up and running again following our June 2 through 5, 2025 maintenance. Thank you for your patience as we performed necessary upgrades and maintenance to keep Unity running smoothly – and welcome back!

Please stay tuned for updates related to the annual maintenance. If you run into any problems, we invite you to join us on our [Unity User Community Slack]({{< relref "community" >}}), where you can get support in the #help-desk channel, or send a ticket to [{{< param "help-email" >}}](mailto:{{< param "help-email" >}}).

## 📩 The University of Rhode Island's AI Lab invites you to their Summer 2025 Workshops
The [University of Rhode Island’s Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their [Summer 2025 workshops](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11oUfCdXM-_HNB37e-IM-VHtEy4ABTHypLZGEGhsNzzI/edit?usp=sharing). The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects. Some workshops start as early as next week – visit our [News page]({{< relref "news/2025/05/uri-summer-25-workshops.md">}}) to see the full schedule and click the Zoom links to register in advance.

## 🗓️ Be sure to attend the URI-presented Introduction to Bioinformatics on Unity Workshop Series
As part of the URI AI Lab’s Summer 2025 Workshops, Unity facilitator Cecile Cres will host an Introduction to Bioinformatics on Unity Workshop series from June 24 through July 24, 2025, at 3:00 - 4:00 PM. See details and register in advance on the [Event page]({{< relref "events/2025/bioinformatics-summer-1.md">}}).
