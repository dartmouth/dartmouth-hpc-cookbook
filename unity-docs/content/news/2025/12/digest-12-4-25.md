## 📦 Introducing the RStor Research Storage System

We’re excited to announce the launch of RStor, a mid-performance, low-cost storage system designed for research data that doesn’t require specialized security controls. It is currently available to **UMass Amherst Groups only**.

RStor is:
- Mounted on the Unity batch cluster via NFS
- Mountable on UMass Amherst desktops via SMB
- Managed through the new [Unity HPC Platform Allocation Portal]({{< param allocation-url >}}), where PIs and groups can request and oversee their storage shares

RStor is hosted at MGHPCC, meaning it will undergo:
- A yearly downtime for a 1-3 days every spring, usually late May or early June
- A monthly maintenance period on the first Tuesday of every month 6-9AM

[Learn more about RStor and find useful resources](https://www.umass.edu/research-computing/rstor).

## ✨ New! The `ood-shared` partition is now live

The `ood-shared` partition is now available for interactive jobs with low utilization. It’s great for code editing, short tests, and other lightweight workloads. This partition includes 21 nodes with 712 cores, with 4x sharing enabled (up to four jobs could share the same core!), providing flexibility and expanded access for quick tasks.
