## ANNOUNCEMENTS 📢

### ⚙️ New hardware alert! L4 Node added to Unity
Unity has been upgraded to include a new node that contains [Nvidia](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/l4/) L4 GPUs. The addition is included on our [Node Features (Constraints)]({{< relref "features" >}}) documentation page.

### 🚀 Our GPU job efficiency tool is available for beta testing!
Test out our GPU job efficiency tool! Example usage: `job_eff_acct.py -u <username> --start=2025-02-01`. Similar to `sacct`, it should default to the current user.

### 👤 Welcome Benjamin Pachev to the Unity Facilitation team!
Benjamin, a Research Fellow at UMass Amherst, received his PhD from the University of Texas at Austin, which focused on improving models of hurricane storm surge and automatic generation of CUDA kernels for solving PDEs. His research interests include GPU computing, optimizing HPC workflows, and automatic code generation for numerical methods. Benjamin loves to run, play board games, and spend time with family in his free time!
