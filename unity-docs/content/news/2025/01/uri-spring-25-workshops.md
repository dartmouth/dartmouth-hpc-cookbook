The [University of Rhode Island's Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their [Spring 2025 workshops](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16pm52QvRBtVoGvNpmem3tB5Q7o1OOj5s44NBoNjDRlk/edit?usp=sharing). The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects.

<!--more-->

For questions regarding the URI AI Lab workshops, contact
[Indrani Mandal](mailto:indrani_mandal@uri.edu?subject=URI%20AI%20Lab%20Workshops).

Join their Meetup Rhode Island AI, or their low-flow listserv for AI Lab updates, programs, and events.
- AI Lab Listserv ([sign up form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScgVdViX-BnjG8jlYLTDmw-ziV4y9fEnw68FR1XZ_1ZHC1vQw/viewform?usp=sf_link)) – receive updates related to the AI Lab, programs and events.
- Rhode Island AI ([Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/URI-AI/)) – receive notification of URI-AI related programs and events.
