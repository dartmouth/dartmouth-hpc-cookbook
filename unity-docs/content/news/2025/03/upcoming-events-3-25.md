## 🗓️ Bioinformatics Workshop Series from the URI AI Lab
The [University of Rhode Island's Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their bioinformatics workshop series, which will offer a practical introduction on how to get started with conducting bioinformatics analyses on Unity. The workshops will guide the participants through the different steps required to run a program on Unity as well as introducing examples of bioinformatics pipelines and software in popular research areas.

**When:** Mon 3/24 - Fri 3/28 at 3-4pm

* Monday, March 24 - Getting started on Unity: Data storage and transfer, submitting jobs, bioinformatics software, and biological datasets.
* Tuesday, March 25 - How to efficiently use the resources on Unity
* Wednesday, March 26 - Amplicon sequencing data analysis
* Thursday, March 27 - Metagenomics data analysis
* Friday, March 28 - Protein structure prediction

[Learn more about the workshops and how to join here.](https://events.uri.edu/event/ai-lab-workshops-bioinformatics-8052)
