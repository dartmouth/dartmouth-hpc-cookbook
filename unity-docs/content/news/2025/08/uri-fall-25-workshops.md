The [University of Rhode Island's Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their [Fall 2025 workshops](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-j1q5D0igeWeUI3BVhhwXiqpEpCRrAQD_qkXx47l0VM/edit?usp=sharing). The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects.

[Indrani Mandal](mailto:indrani_mandal@uri.edu?subject=URI%20AI%20Lab%20Workshops).

Join their AI Lab Listserv ([sign up form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScgVdViX-BnjG8jlYLTDmw-ziV4y9fEnw68FR1XZ_1ZHC1vQw/viewform?usp=sf_link)) to receive updates related to the AI Lab, programs and events.
