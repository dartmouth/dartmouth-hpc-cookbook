## ANNOUNCEMENTS 📢
### ⚙️ Two additional GH200 "Grace Hopper" servers have been purchased by UMass Amherst Research Computing!
We’re excited to announce that two additional GH200 "Grace Hopper" servers are now available in the `arm-gpu` partition, joining the existing server purchased by URI. Please note that these machines are based on the ARM architecture, so your code may require modification to run on them.
