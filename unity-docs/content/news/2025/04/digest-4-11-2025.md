Hi Unity Users! Keep reading for a few announcements about Unity!

## ANNOUNCEMENTS 📢

### ⏰Annual shutdown will begin on June 2nd at 9:00PM
We are approaching our annual maintenance period, where Unity will be completely offline starting on June 2nd, at 9:00PM, for necessary maintenance and upgrades. We expect to be back online by 9:00AM on June 5th, if not earlier. This coincides with the annual shutdown at the datacenter Unity lives at, the MGHPCC.

We encourage you to keep an eye out on our [Unity User Community Slack]({{< relref "community" >}}) where you can receive reminders about the shutdown and get the most up-to-date information from the Unity team.

### 🗓️ Thank you to everyone who attended HPC Day 2025 on April 4th!
HPC Day 2025 is a wrap – thank you to everyone who attended and contributed to a great event!
