## ANNOUNCEMENTS

### ⏰ New VS Code Session Limit – 1 per user
We will be limiting VS Code sessions on Unity OnDemand to one per user.

### 🎊 Harmony has been installed!
At the end of 2024, the Unity team received an NSF grant to add [Harmony](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2430001). Last week, Harmony was officially installed at the [MGHPCC](https://www.mghpcc.org/)! If you're interested in getting involved — as a student worker, beta tester, or simply to receive updates when deployment is complete — please fill out this form: https://forms.office.com/r/phehJTxHww. 

[Learn more about Harmony](https://www.umass.edu/news/article/research-computing-data-team-umass-amherst-awarded-11-million-design-new-energy). 

### 🚨 Scratch workspace: 15TB quota
We have now changed the scratch storage quota to 15TB. For extreme scratch needs, reach out to the Unity team.

### ⚠️ We encourage users to migrate data from `/scratch/workspace` to `/scratch3/workspace`
Due to space constraints on `/scratch/workspace`, we are asking users who are expecting large or rapid growth of their scratch space to consider migrating data to `/scratch3/workspace`. You can do this by creating a new workspace, which will automatically be created there, and moving some data over. We are currently working on buying another petabyte of scratch!

### 🎉 Unity ran its 40 MILLIONTH job!
Unity’s 40 millionth job was a very short array job task that started on Tuesday, July 15th!

## REMINDERS 💡

### 📩 The University of Rhode Island's AI Lab invites you to their Summer 2025 Workshops
The [University of Rhode Island’s Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their [Summer 2025 workshops](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11oUfCdXM-_HNB37e-IM-VHtEy4ABTHypLZGEGhsNzzI/edit?usp=sharing). The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects. Visit our [News page]({{< relref "news/2025/05/uri-summer-25-workshops.md">}}) to see the full schedule and click the Zoom links to register in advance.

### 🗓️ Be sure to attend the URI-presented Introduction to Bioinformatics on Unity Workshop Series
As part of the URI AI Lab’s Summer 2025 Workshops, Unity facilitator Cecile Cres will host an Introduction to Bioinformatics on Unity Workshop series from June 24 through July 24, 2025, at 3:00 - 4:00 PM. See details and register in advance on the [Event page]({{< relref "events/2025/bioinformatics-summer-3.md">}}).
