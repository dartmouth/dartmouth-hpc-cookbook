## ANNOUNCEMENTS 📢

### 🗓️ UPCOMING WORKSHOP: You Don't Need Sudo: An Introduction to Installing Software on Unity
On Wednesday, May 28th at 2pm, Unity Facilitator Benjamin Pachev will lead a workshop on installing software on Unity. Whether you're completely new to Unity, or are already an experienced user, this workshop is for you! We'll cover the basics of Unity software installation, walk through some sample installs from real Unity users, and share advanced tips and tricks that can help you deal with even the most challenging installations.

If you’re planning to attend, please [RSVP here](https://umass-amherst.zoom.us/meeting/register/plBUfZMySa2wPVtFgiIWuQ).

### ⏰ REMINDER: Annual shutdown will begin on June 2nd at 9:00 PM
We are approaching our annual maintenance period, where Unity will be completely offline starting on June 2nd, at 9:00 PM, for necessary maintenance and upgrades. We expect to be back online by 9AM, June 5, if not earlier. This coincides with the annual shutdown at the datacenter Unity lives at, the MGHPCC.

We encourage you to keep an eye out on our [Unity User Community Slack](https://docs.unity.rc.umass.edu/contact/community/) where you can receive reminders about the shutdown and get the most up-to-date information from the Unity team.

### ⚠️ REMINDER: `/project` and `/nese` storage will be going offline at 4:00 PM on 6/2
Ahead of the annual shutdown, `/project` and `/nese` storage will be going offline at 4:00 PM on June 2, 2025, due to the timing of the NESE storage team’s shutdown. We encourage you to plan accordingly!
