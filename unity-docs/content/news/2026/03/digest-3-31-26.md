## ⏱️ OOD Activity Now Keeps Your Account Active

Using Open OnDemand (OOD) will now reset your account’s idle timer, meaning activity in OOD helps prevent account expiration, not just portal logins.

## ⚙️ Policy Update: `hpc-workspace` Extensions

Extensions in `hpc-workspace` are now effectively unlimited due to a recent policy change.
