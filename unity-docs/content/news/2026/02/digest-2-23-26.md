## ⚠️ Important: Account Expiration Beginning April 1, 2026
Our automated account expiration process is going into effect on **April 1, 2026**. Beginning April 1, any account that has not logged into the [account portal](https://account.unityhpc.org/) in the **past 180 days** will be idle-locked. Idle-locked users are unauthorized to log in via SSH or to use the Open OnDemand and Globus web interfaces.

If your account becomes idle-locked, simply log in to the [account portal](https://account.unityhpc.org/) to restore access. You will receive a confirmation message once your lock has been removed. Please note that it may take **up to 10 minutes** for your status to be updated across all Unity systems.

To learn more about our account expiration process, see our [policy]({{< relref "about/account-expiration.md">}}).

## 🆕 New Command for Account Usage
`squota` is a new command available that allows you to see your individual account usage across all the scratch file systems.
