Hi Unity Users! This week’s digest includes updates to nodes and AI dataset directories, a recent milestone, and reminders about upcoming learning opportunities.
<!--more-->

## 🆕 Newly Added & Updated Nodes
We’ve recently made the following updates to nodes:
* Added `uri-cpu[074-102]` to `cpu-preempt` (these are single power supply nodes, so jobs could be interrupted)
* Added `uri-gpu[015-017]` to `gpu` (two with 2x A100-40 and one with 4x A100-40)
* Reconfigured `gpu047` to have 2x A4000 (16GB VRAM)

## ⚠️ AI Dataset Update: `/datasets/ai/qwen` Directory Changes by Feb. 13
As the number of Qwen models continues to grow, we will be splitting the existing directory `/datasets/ai/qwen` into three directories by **February 13**: `/datasets/ai/qwen`,` /datasets/ai/qwen2`, and `/datasets/ai/qwen3`.

Please be sure to update your job paths accordingly.

## 🎉 Unity Community Slack `#help-desk` Channel Reaches 2,000 Users!

Last week, the Unity Community Slack’s `#help-desk` channel hit a milestone of **2,000 users**! Welcome to everyone who has recently joined, and thank you to all who help make this such a supportive and active community.

## 📩 Reminder: URI AI Lab’s Spring 2026 Workshops

Starting this week, the University of Rhode Island’s [Artificial Intelligence (URI AI) Lab](https://events.uri.edu/group/ai) invites the Unity community to attend their Spring 2026 Workshops. The URI AI Lab is dedicated to interdisciplinary research and learning in the context of use-inspired AI. The Lab runs workshops on common AI tools and offers consultancy for research and educational projects. Visit our [News page]({{< relref "news/2026/01/uri-spring-26-workshops.md">}}) to see the full schedule and click the Zoom links to register in advance.

## 🗓️ Reminder: NEW Weekly Workshop on Quantum Algorithms

As part of the URI AI Lab’s [Spring 2026 Workshops]({{< relref "news/2026/01/uri-spring-26-workshops.md">}}), URI is hosting a new weekly workshop called [Quantum Algorithms for Engineers and Scientists](https://events.uri.edu/event/quantum-algorithms-for-engineers-and-scientist) that will introduce quantum computing and quantum algorithms for a general audience.

The workshop series takes place weekly on **Fridays, starting on January 30, from 12:30 - 2:00pm**. See details and register in advance on the [Event page](https://events.uri.edu/event/quantum-algorithms-for-engineers-and-scientist).
